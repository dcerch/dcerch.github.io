Overview

This name generator is an adaptation of Brian David Gilbert's Kojima Name Generator. The original 11-page worksheet used to create a Kojima name is now a single tactical app — slightly modified to automate the process. You do not need to figure out name alternatives, conditions, or categories. Just fill out the Personal and Kojima information; the codec handles the rest.

Instructions

Complete both transmissions below — PERSONAL INTEL and KOJIMA INTEL — then transmit GENERATE CODENAME to receive your designation instantly. Your intel is stored locally on this device only.
