Everybody is John!

A competitive game of voices & willpower
How to Play

The Pitch

Everyone but the Game Master plays a Voice in the head of John — a hapless man who can barely tie his shoes. Each Voice is obsessed with making John do one specific thing, and spends a limited pool of willpower to seize control and pull it off.

Setup

Each Voice starts with 10 willpower and 2 skills. You may spend 3 willpower up front (starting at 7 instead) to gain a 3rd skill. Pick an obsession and rate how hard it is: 1 ★ easy 2 ★★ risky 3 ★★★ near-impossible — that's how many points it scores.

Taking Control

When John wakes, gets hurt, the active Voice fails a roll, or a Voice completes its obsession, a control test happens. Every interested Voice secretly bids willpower. Highest bid wins and pays it; ties roll off. Everyone else keeps their willpower.

Doing Things

When John attempts something risky, the active Voice rolls 1d6. With a matching skill you need 3+; without one you need a 6. Before rolling you may spend willpower for +1 each — enough makes it automatic. Fail and control is up for grabs again.

Scoring & The End

Complete your obsession to score its point value, then a new control test begins. If John naps (GM's call), every Voice gains +1 willpower. The game ends when John dies or all Voices run out of willpower — highest score wins.

Assemble the Voices

Add 3 to 6 Voices, then start.
