🧃 J2 tips & tricks
  • Layer the hopper bottom → top: leafy greens first, then soft produce, then medium, then hard items on top. The weight of the hard stuff pushes the greens down into the auger.
  • Cut leafy greens (kale, chard, collards) into thirds and celery into thirds so long fibres don't wrap around the blade.
  • Pair greens with cucumber, celery or apple — watery produce flushes leafy pulp through and boosts yield.
  • Skip the juice strainer for banana, avocado and mango — they turn to paste. Save them for smoothies or the sorbet attachment.
  • Foam is normal with greens and apples. Stir it back in or skim it off.
  • Pomegranate: arils only. Pit stone fruit and dates. Core apples and pears (seeds out).
  • Run time: keep sessions to about 20 minutes, then let the motor rest.
  • Clean right away before pulp dries. Running REV for ~15 seconds helps loosen parts for disassembly; an overnight baking-soda soak deep-cleans the strainer.
  • Save your pulp: freeze it in ice-cube trays for smoothies, muffins, crackers or broth — or compost it.
  • Storage: airtight glass jar filled to the very top, fridge, best within 24–48 h (72 h max). A squeeze of lemon slows browning.
