Lost at Sea ⛵

Castaway in the mid-Atlantic — rank what keeps you alive.

🏝️ Situation Report

You chartered a yacht with three friends for the trip of a lifetime across the Atlantic Ocean. Since none of you had sailing experience, you hired an experienced skipper and a two-person crew. About halfway through your journey, a fierce fire breaks out in the ship's galley. The skipper and crew are lost fighting the blaze. Much of the yacht is destroyed and slowly sinking. Your location is unclear — the navigational and radio equipment were damaged in the fire. Your best estimate is that you are many hundreds of miles from land.

You and your friends managed to save 15 items, undamaged after the fire. In addition, you salvaged a four-man rubber life raft and a box of matches. Your task is to rank the 15 items by their importance to your survival as you wait to be rescued.

⏳ When you press Set Sail, a 4-minute timer begins. Drag items — or use the ▲▼ buttons — to order them from most to least important. Lock in any time with Finish Early, or the list finalizes automatically when time runs out. Then see whether you'd survive, your odds of rescue, and how you stack up against the US Coast Guard.

    📜 Quick Explanation

    According to the US Coast Guard, the basic supplies needed when stranded mid-ocean are articles to attract attention and aid survival until rescue arrives. A transatlantic trip takes roughly 20 days — significantly less with good winds, far more without them.

    Articles for navigation are of little importance: even if a small raft could reach land, it would be impossible to store enough food and water to last that long. Without signaling devices, there is almost no chance of being spotted and rescued.
