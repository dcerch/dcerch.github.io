3D assets used by Navigator
===========================

assets/models/*   assets/textures/*   assets/hdri/*

All models, textures and the outdoor panorama come from Poly Haven
(https://polyhaven.com) and are licensed CC0 1.0 (public domain):
https://polyhaven.com/license
No attribution is required, but thank you, Poly Haven!

- Models: furniture and props (Sofa_01, ArmChair_01, Television_01, electric_stove,
  GreenChair_01, vintage_grandfather_clock_01, treasure_chest, ...). The full list
  is in tools/fetch_assets.py.
- Textures: parquet, plaster, brick, velour, brown leather, kitchen wood, marble.
- Panorama: "Noon Grass" (noon_grass), the tonemapped JPG downscaled to 4k/2k and
  shown through every window via three.js GroundedSkybox.

Model colour maps are resized to 1024px (other maps 512px) by tools/fetch_assets.py.
Re-run that script to fetch them again or to add more (edit the MODELS / TEXTURES lists).
