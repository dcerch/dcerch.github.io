3D assets used by Navigator
===========================

assets/models/*   and   assets/textures/*

All models and textures come from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com) and are
licensed CC0 1.0 (public domain): https://polyhaven.com/license
No attribution is required, but thank you, Poly Haven!

They were downloaded and their textures resized to 512px (models) / 1024px
(floors) for the web by tools/fetch_assets.py. Re-run that script to fetch them
again or to add more (edit the MODELS / TEXTURES lists).
